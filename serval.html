You've written a Head of EMEA job.

Your posting describes building the European go-to-market from nothing. Four sub-regions. A strategy you set rather than inherit. Partnership with Sales on deal strategy, not event execution. A team you'll hire as scope and budget grow.

The title on it is Senior Field Marketing & ABM Manager. Those are two different jobs, and I think the gap is worth ten minutes of your time — whether or not it ends with me.

The part about the title

Said plainly

I'm not going to pretend the level doesn't matter to me. I'm Head of EMEA Marketing at DigiCert and I'm not looking to move backwards. I'd rather say that than have you work it out later.

But that's the smaller half of the argument. The bigger half is commercial.

In a region where almost nobody has heard of you yet, your first marketing hire's title is a go-to-market asset. A Senior Field Marketing Manager has a hard time convening a room of CIOs in Frankfurt. A Head of EMEA Marketing gets the meeting. The title isn't recognition — it's access, and access is most of what you're hiring for.

Under-titling a founding regional hire is cheap in month one and expensive in month eighteen, when you either promote them or replace them.

So I'd want the job your posting describes, at a level that matches it. If that isn't where the role sits, fair enough — I'd still rather have told you what I think of it than quietly not applied.
